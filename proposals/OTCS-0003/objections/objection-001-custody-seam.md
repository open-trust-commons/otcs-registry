# Objection 001 — the registry is revising a vocabulary it does not hold

**Raised:** 2026-09-13 (self-raised; the calendar's own note on this proposal
said the ownership seam "should be named inside 0003's deliberation record
rather than discovered after ratification").

**Objection.** `CHARTER.md` §2 gives KTP custody of the vocabulary and the
underlying model, and §2a says custody must not become interpretive authority
over registered architectures. OTCS-0003 renames two coordinates, relocates a
third out of the vector, and adds a layer — through OTCS's process, decided by
OTCS's participants. Either OTCS is exceeding its authority, or the boundary in
§2 does not mean what it says.

**Answer (2026-09-13).** The boundary holds because of what this proposal does
not do. KTP's custody is over **meanings**: what `execute` means, what
`environment` measures, what a maturity level asserts. This proposal changes
**none** of them — every value list is untouched, and the compatibility mapping
is rename-or-relocate at every row. OTCS's authority is over the **registry's
use** of the vocabulary: which field a value sits in, what the field is called,
how many layers a record has. That is what changes here. Layer 2 is new and is
not KTP's at all; it is adopted from Tamed Autonomy and attributed. Had this
proposal changed a meaning, §2a would require the custodian on the record, and
the founder holding both roles could not have supplied that. The line is
therefore drawn, and recorded, at *meaning*: any future proposal that crosses
it needs the custodian as a participant, not as the same person wearing a
second hat.

**Status:** answered; carried on the record. **Ruling confirmed by the founder
2026-09-19** and binding on future proposals: one that changes a meaning needs
the custodian as a participant, not the same person in a second hat.
