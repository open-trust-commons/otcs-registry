# OTCS-0001 — Adopt the v0.1 registry seed records

Adopts the seven seed records in `registry/projects/`: three registered
(founder-maintained, honest maturity profiles, independent validation 0) and four
fictional example records covering enforcement, observability, evidence, and
repair.

**Clock demonstration:** this is a `registry_update` proposal with a 3–7 day
minimum deliberation clock (GOVERNANCE.md §3), started 2026-07-25. Unlike
OTCS-0000, whose bootstrap waiver was disclosed as unavoidable, this proposal
**waits its clock out**: ballots are cast, but no decision record may be written
before 2026-07-28 at the earliest. The clocks bind the founder or they bind
nobody.
