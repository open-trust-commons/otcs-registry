# Code of conduct

*Version 0.1 · Status: EXPERIMENTAL*

**This registry exists to coordinate people who disagree.** Disagreement is protected here. What follows governs how it is conducted, not whether it is permitted.

---

## 1. The seven rules

**1. Critique claims, not people.**
"This edge has no evidence behind it" is the system working as designed. Speculation about someone's motives, competence or character is not.

**2. Bring evidence.**
An objection carries a source or a line of reasoning. *"I don't like it"* is voice — everyone has it, always — but it is not an objection the record has to answer.

**3. Disclose rather than hide.**
Conflicts of interest are normal. Disclosed ones are **data**. Undisclosed ones, once exposed, are a conduct issue. The project does not hunt for purity; it asks you to say what you are.

**4. Correction is honoured.**
Correcting your own earlier claim **earns standing** ([VOTING.md](VOTING.md) §8). Punishing somebody for doing it is itself a violation.

**5. No harassment.**
No personal attacks, doxxing, intimidation or sustained unwanted contact — **in any channel, canonical or not** ([COMMUNICATIONS.md](COMMUNICATIONS.md)).

**6. No impersonation.**
Of people, of projects, or of roles.

**7. Use the process in good faith.**
Disputes, objections and emergency reports are tools. Demonstrably vexatious use of them is a conduct issue.

## 2. Reporting

Reports go to the **private plane** ([COMMUNICATIONS.md](COMMUNICATIONS.md) §6) — one of the few things private channels are legitimately for.

## 3. Who enforces

| Stage | Who |
|---|---|
| **Stage 1** (now) | The maintainer, on the public record wherever safety allows ([MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) §1) |
| **Stage 2 onward** | A designated conduct group |

Outcomes are recorded in the governance ledger, redacted where a person's safety requires it.

**Redacted is not the same as absent.** A conduct action that left no trace at all would be exactly the invisible governance this project refuses ([COMMUNICATIONS.md](COMMUNICATIONS.md) §7).
