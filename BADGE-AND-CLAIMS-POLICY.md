# What you may say about being listed

*Version 0.1 · Status: EXPERIMENTAL*

**There is no badge. There will never be a badge.**

No "OTCS Certified" logo, no seal, no mark, no shield, no scorecard graphic.

> A badge compresses a record into a symbol — and compression into symbols is exactly how this field manufactures authority it has not earned.

---

## 1. The distinctions that must never collapse

```text
Listed in OTCS
    ≠ Validated by OTCS
    ≠ Conforming to KTP
    ≠ Independently tested
    ≠ Safe to deploy
```

## 2. You may say

- "Registered in the Open Trust Commons"
- "Our OTCS record is at ‹link›"
- "We claim to implement the ‹name› interface at rung ‹rung›" — **as your record states**
- "We use OTCS schemas"
- Quote or cite the specifications, under CC BY 4.0

## 3. You may not say

| Not permitted | |
|---|---|
| "OTCS certified" · "OTCS approved" · "OTCS validated" | No such status exists |
| "Verified by the Open Trust Commons" | Verification is of **owner control**, not of a project's quality ([OWNER-VERIFICATION.md](OWNER-VERIFICATION.md)) |
| Any phrasing implying endorsement, accreditation or a passed evaluation | None of those happen here |
| A compatibility rung you have not reached | — |

> **`CONFORMANCE_TESTED` is currently unreachable, because no conformance suites exist.** Nobody can truthfully claim it today, whatever their record says.

## 4. Enforcement

**First, a record correction.** The project's own record states the truth, publicly.

**Second, a conduct matter** ([ACCEPTABLE-USE.md](ACCEPTABLE-USE.md) §1).

Persistent misrepresentation is grounds for a `DISPUTED` state on the affected claims — **visible, appealable, never silent.**

## 5. Why this is strict

> **The registry's only asset is that its statements mean exactly what they say.**

A badge would be worth more to a vendor than the record is. That is the definition of a corruptible incentive, and this document exists to keep it from ever arising.
