# Keeping this from becoming a weapon

*Version 0.1 · Status: EXPERIMENTAL · Companion to [PRIVACY.md](privacy.md)*

**A provenance system can become a surveillance system.** That is not a hypothetical worry bolted on afterwards — it is inherent to the design, and these boundaries exist because of it.

---

## 1. The risk, stated plainly

This registry records relationships between people and projects, disputes, affiliations, funding ties and evaluator-independence disclosures.

> **The same machinery that makes provenance legible can assemble a profile of a person or mount a campaign against them.**

## 2. The rules

**Collect the minimum.** A record carries the least personal detail its stated purpose requires. Declaring a conflict of interest requires **the relationship** — never a person's private life.

**No profile assembly.** Records must not be used, or arranged, to build dossiers on individuals. **A submission whose evident purpose is targeting a person is refused** ([ACCEPTABLE-USE.md](acceptable-use.md) §1).

**A right to respond.** Anyone named in a disputed record may submit a response, which **attaches to the record rather than replacing it.** Responses are not gated on a maintainer agreeing with them.

**Redaction.** Personal details may be redacted on request while the project-level facts they were attached to survive. Redaction is recorded as an event — **the fact of a redaction is public even when its content is not.**

**Minors.** No record may name a minor. Where a project is maintained by one, only the contact the project itself published appears.

**Security researchers choose their attribution.** Sensitive vulnerability evidence is not published while remediation is open, and may be **withheld permanently** where publication would endanger users ([SECURITY.md](security.md) §5).

**Volume is not evidence.** A flood of low-quality submissions aimed at burying someone is a conduct matter, not a research contribution.

**Legal threats.** Threats used to suppress legitimate review are a conduct violation. Lawful takedown requests are honoured where required, and **disclosed in the transparency record where that is permitted.**

## 3. Escalation

Safety matters go to the **private channel** ([COMMUNICATIONS.md](communications.md) §6), not to public issues.

Emergency removal is available. It is recorded in the log and **followed by a public, redacted account of what authority acted and why** ([ACCEPTABLE-USE.md](acceptable-use.md) §5).
