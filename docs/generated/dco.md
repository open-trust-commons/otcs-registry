# Developer Certificate of Origin

*Version 0.1 · Status: EXPERIMENTAL*

**You keep your copyright. The project gets a clear right to distribute what you sent.**

That is the whole trade. There is no copyright-assignment agreement to sign, and no lawyer required to read this page.

---

## 1. How you sign off

```bash
git commit -s -m "your message"
```

The `-s` appends one line to your commit:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

## 2. What that line certifies

Five things:

- **You wrote it**, or you have the right to submit it under the licences below
- **Code and schemas** are contributed under **Apache-2.0**
- **Specification and documentation text** is contributed under **CC BY 4.0**
- **You disclosed where it came from** — including AI assistance, where that assistance is material to the substance of the contribution ([AI-USE.md](ai-use.md))
- **You understand this is public and permanent.** Your contribution and your sign-off become part of the record

## 3. What it does not do

| It does not… | Because |
|---|---|
| Transfer ownership | You keep your copyright |
| Make you a maintainer | That has its own process ([MAINTAINERS.md](maintainers.md) §4) |
| Grant governance authority | Authority comes only from the published structure ([VOTING.md](voting.md) §1) |
| Make your contribution canonical | Incorporation and canonical status are separate decisions ([GOVERNANCE.md](governance.md) §7) |

**Nor does it erase your provenance.** An accepted contribution stays recorded with its author, permanently — including contributions that were later superseded or reversed.
