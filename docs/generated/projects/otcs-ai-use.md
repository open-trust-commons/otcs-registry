## In its own words

A registry built partly in response to a field flooded with AI-generated framework material has to make its own use of AI legible, or the criticism is hollow. This policy says what AI may be used for inside the registry, the conditions attached, and what it may never do.



**Known limitations, as stated by the project:** Enforced by disclosure and review, not by a gate in any system: a contributor who does not disclose is caught by review or not at all. No independent party has evaluated compliance. The policy governs the registry's own surfaces only; it says nothing about how any listed project uses AI.
