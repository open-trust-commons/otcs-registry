# What gets published about people

*Version 0.1 · Status: EXPERIMENTAL*

This registry publishes information about projects — and therefore about **people**, organisations, affiliations, relationships and disputes. That obliges rules.

---

## 1. Published

- Maintainer names and roles, **as supplied by the project**
- Canonical URLs
- Declared affiliations, where they are relevant to a conflict-of-interest disclosure
- Relationship and claim records
- Governance participation — **ballots carry a voter identity and a disclosed affiliation by design** ([VOTING.md](voting.md) §7)

## 2. Not published

- Personal contact details beyond a contact address the project itself supplied
- Home addresses
- Personal phone numbers
- Employment history beyond a declared affiliation
- **Any personal data a record's stated purpose does not require**

> Declaring a conflict of interest requires **the relationship**. It never requires a person's private life.

## 3. Correction and removal

Anyone named in a record may request correction or removal of **personal details.**

**Project-level facts — dates, artifacts, citations — are not removable on that basis.** That line is the same one [EVIDENCE-MODEL.md](evidence-model.md) §1 draws between what somebody asserts and what the evidence shows.

- Corrections are **new records**
- History is annotated, **never rewritten**
- Affiliation changes are recorded going forward, not erased retroactively

## 4. Harassment and doxxing

Records must not be used to assemble profiles of individuals, and nothing whose evident purpose is targeting a person will be hosted here.

Conduct rules are in [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](code-of-conduct.md). Reports go to the private channel ([COMMUNICATIONS.md](communications.md) §6).

## 5. Projects that never asked to be here

An `observed` record describes a **project**, from public evidence, and is **never presented as that project's participation.**

> **If maintainers ask that no record exist in their name, that is honoured** ([CHARTER.md](charter.md) §10).
