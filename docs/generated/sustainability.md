# Money

*Version 0.1 · Status: EXPERIMENTAL · **Written before there is any**, which is the only time these rules can be written honestly*

---

## 1. The costs that will arrive

Domain · archival services · legal and patent review · security audit · accessibility work · meeting infrastructure · **maintainer time.**

## 2. The eight rules

**1 · No pay-to-play placement.** Position, prominence and inclusion are never purchasable.

**2 · No paid evidence status.** A vendor cannot buy `INDEPENDENTLY_TESTED`, a maturity level, or a compatibility rung. **This is the rule the whole registry stands on.**

**3 · Sponsors cannot influence classification.** Not records, not evidence states, not relationship status, not proposal outcomes.

**4 · Sponsorships are disclosed** — amount band, source and date, in the public record.

**5 · Evaluator compensation is disclosed**, and a paid evaluator's independence is assessed under [EVIDENCE-MODEL.md](evidence-model.md) §3 like any other relationship.

**6 · Financial reports are public** the moment any money moves.

**7 · Paid services, if they ever exist, stay separated from registry truth.** A service may help someone prepare a record. **It may never affect how that record is evaluated.**

**8 · Grants buy no governance authority.** No ballot, no seat, no veto ([VOTING.md](voting.md) §1).

## 3. Where this stands structurally

There is **deliberately no legal entity** at version 0.x ([NON-GOALS.md](non-goals.md)). A fiscal host or foundation is a Stage 4 question ([CHARTER.md](charter.md) §7).

Until then the costs are borne by the founding maintainer and disclosed as such — **which is itself a conflict, declared like any other** ([MAINTAINERS.md](maintainers.md) §1).

## 4. The test

> **If a funding arrangement would make any sentence in [EVIDENCE-MODEL.md](evidence-model.md) less true, it is refused — regardless of amount.**
