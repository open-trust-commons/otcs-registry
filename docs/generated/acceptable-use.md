# What may enter the record

*Version 0.1 · Status: EXPERIMENTAL*

[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](code-of-conduct.md) governs how people treat each other. **This governs what is allowed into the record itself.**

---

## 1. Not acceptable

- Spam and commercial solicitation
- Impersonating a person, a project or a role
- Repetitive low-value submissions
- Harassment
- **False or fabricated evidence**
- Fabricated endorsements
- Undisclosed conflicts of interest
- Discriminatory conduct
- **Legal threats used to suppress legitimate review**
- **Weaponised provenance claims** — filing a derivation or priority claim to damage a competitor rather than to correct a record

> The last two matter most here. **A provenance system attracts people who want to use provenance as a weapon.** The rules have to name that before it happens rather than after.

## 2. Who moderates

| Stage | Who |
|---|---|
| **Stage 1** (now) | The founding maintainer, on the public record wherever safety permits |
| **From Stage 2** | A designated conduct group, **with no single organisation holding a majority** ([SECURITY.md](security.md) §4) |

## 3. Action requires evidence

Proportional to severity, in this order:

```text
warning
  → temporary suspension of submission privileges
     → removal of the offending record
        → sustained suspension
```

Each step is recorded in the log with its stated grounds.

## 4. Appeal

**Every moderation action is appealable**, and an appeal escalates to the next governance stage rather than ending with the person who acted.

> **Wrongful moderation is corrected in public, and the correction is recorded.** The record shows the action *and* its reversal. There is no clean slate.

## 5. Emergency removal

Available for **active harm** — doxxing, exposed credentials, active exploitation.

Scoped, recorded in the log, and **expires automatically after 7 days** unless ratified through the normal process ([GOVERNANCE.md](governance.md) §8).

## 6. The transparency report

Published with every release: counts by category · actions taken · appeals · reversals.

> **A moderation system that does not report on itself is asking for trust it refuses to evidence.**
