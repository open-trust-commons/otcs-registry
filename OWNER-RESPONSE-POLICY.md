# Your right to answer back

*Version 0.1 · Status: EXPERIMENTAL*

**Before an analysis naming your project is published, you see the draft.**

---

## 1. What you can do with it

- Confirm it
- Qualify it
- Object to it
- Supply evidence
- Publish a response alongside it

**This is not a veto.** You cannot suppress a source-grounded finding by declining to agree with it.

> **One exception:** material that is confidential or legally prohibited must be removed or redacted — and the redaction itself is recorded.

## 2. Your response is permanent

> **An owner's response stays visible even when the analyst does not adopt it.**

That is the centre of this policy. A registry that publishes findings but not the subject's rebuttal is a publication, not a commons.

- Responses attach to the analysis
- **Nobody but the author edits a response**
- A response is never quietly dropped when the analysis is superseded — the successor carries it forward, or explains why it did not

## 3. The possible states

```text
confirmed
confirmed_with_qualification
disputed
no_response          (recorded with the date the window opened)
declined_to_respond
```

**`no_response` is recorded neutrally.** Silence is not agreement, and the record says so rather than letting a reader infer either way.

## 4. Timing

You get a stated window before publication, scaled to the class of analysis.

A missed window publishes with `no_response` — and **the path stays open indefinitely.** A late response is attached whenever it arrives, however long that takes.

## 5. Corrections

A factual correction you supply and the analyst accepts produces a **new version** of the analysis. The prior version is superseded and preserved.

> **Corrections are never applied by editing the published record in place** ([ANALYSIS-MODEL.md](ANALYSIS-MODEL.md) §7).
